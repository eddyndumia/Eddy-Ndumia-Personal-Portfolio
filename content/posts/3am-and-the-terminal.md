---
title: "3am and the Terminal"
date: 2026-09-18
---

There's a version of me that only shows up at 3am. Everyone's asleep, the house is actually quiet for once, and it's just me and whatever bug decided tonight was the night it would finally show up. I used to file this under bad time management. Now I think it's just the one hour left where nothing's asking anything of me except the problem in front of me.

Debugging at that hour is close to meditative if I'm honest. No Slack, no calls, nobody checking in. Just logs, waiting for me to actually read them instead of guessing and hoping. Put in the careful hour, you find the off-by-one. Skip it because you're tired and it's late, it finds you tomorrow instead, usually worse, usually in front of someone.

I won't call it romantic, it's mostly just tired. But there's an honesty in tired work that rested work doesn't really have. You can't fake understanding a stack trace to yourself at 3am. Either you see it or you don't. Maybe that's the real reason I keep ending up back here, past the hour I told myself I'd stop.

Some people find their quiet on a run, or praying, or staring at water till their head empties out. Mine's a black terminal at an hour normal people are supposed to be asleep. Not sure that's healthy. Pretty sure it's honest. Right now, honest is the version of myself I trust the most.
