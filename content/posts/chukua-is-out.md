title: "Chukua Is Out"
date: 2026-10-01T19:00:00+03:00
---

A week ago I wrote about how Nairobi is full of free stuff nobody can find. The sofa outside the gate after a move, the cot in the store, the twenty office chairs nobody knows what to do with. And somebody a few kilometres away who needs exactly that thing.

Chukua is out now. Version 1.0.0, Android, you can download it from [the site](https://chukua.ndumiaeddy8.workers.dev).

It's small and that's on purpose. You list something in about thirty seconds, people near you see it, someone claims it and comes for it. Your number stays private until someone has claimed. If the item is still there on the day you said it had to be gone, the listing just disappears.

A few things changed since that first post. Sign-in is now a code sent to your email instead of SMS. SMS costs money per message and we're running this on close to nothing, email doesn't. Givers add a phone number when they list, only so the person who claims can call about pickup.

Claims are free for now. The plan was always a small M-Pesa fee per claim, so people only claim what they'll actually pick up, and that will come. But at the start the problem isn't flaky takers, it's an empty feed. So for the pilot nobody pays anything, we just want things moving from one house to another.

The test version had four hundred demo listings so the feed looked alive. Those are gone. What's on there now is real, and right now that's not much. That's the honest state of it. Every marketplace starts empty and the only way out is one listing at a time.

So if you're in Nairobi and you have something in the house that works and nobody uses, put it on there. A microwave, a chair, a box of books. Someone near you will probably take it. And if you're looking for something, check what's near you every few days, it changes.

It's Android only for now. iPhone later if this works.

If something breaks or feels off, tell me. That's the whole point of putting it out early.
