I made a small game called Aura Farm. It was an experiment more than anything, I don't think much of it as a product.

Slang falls from the top of the screen, you catch the good ones and dodge the bad ones, and your character changes the longer you last. The characters are the Italian brainrot ones kids keep talking about. Those images are AI-made and public domain, which is the only reason they're in there.

The real reason I built it was to learn a few things. What a small ad-supported game actually makes, if anything. What Google Play asks for before an app can go public. How to handle a game that kids will play, which meant adding an age check and only showing non-personalised ads to anyone under 13.

It's TypeScript on a plain canvas, no engine, so the same code runs in the browser and as an Android app. No account, nothing leaves your phone.

You can play it in the browser or get the Android app from GitHub. The Play Store version needs fourteen days of testing first, so that comes around mid October. If you want to be a tester, send me your Gmail.

[Play Aura Farm](https://aura-farm.ndumiaeddy8.workers.dev/play/) if you're curious.
