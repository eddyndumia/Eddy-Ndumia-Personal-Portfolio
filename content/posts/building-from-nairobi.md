---
title: "Building From Nairobi While Everyone Else Talks About San Francisco"
date: 2026-09-15
---

Every founder story I read growing up had the same backdrop. Some garage, some campus, some accelerator with a name everyone's heard of. Meanwhile I'm building PesaScore off M-Pesa statements, a payment rail that most of the people writing those stories have literally never used, for a market they've never priced anything for.

Nobody's watching this build the way people watch a YC demo day. There's no hype cycle checking in on whether a SACCO in Nairobi actually wants a better way to score a loan applicant who doesn't have a credit bureau file. That's honestly fine, mostly. It means nobody's going to overreact to a slow week, but it also means there's no external pressure forcing the pace either. You have to actually want it enough to keep going without an audience.

The thing that doesn't translate from the usual startup content is that "alternative data" here isn't a buzzword, it's the only data that exists for a huge chunk of people. There's no bureau file to fall back on for someone paying rent and buying stock for their kiosk entirely through M-Pesa. The alternative data IS the data. That's not a scrappy underdog narrative, it's just what building for this market actually requires, and most of the frameworks and blog posts about credit scoring assume a bureau file exists in the first place.

I'm not saying this to complain, I actually think it's the more interesting problem. Just noting that half the startup playbook doesn't apply, and you find that out by hitting the wall, not by reading about it first.
