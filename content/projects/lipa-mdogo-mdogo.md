A Node/Express backend for a pay-as-you-go ("lipa mdogo mdogo") financing model — the kind of setup behind installment plans for phones or other big-ticket items, where someone pays in smaller pieces over time instead of one lump sum upfront. Models, controllers, and routes for tracking the plan itself and the repayments against it.

**Tech stack:** Node.js, Express

**Repo:** [lipa-mdogo-mdogo](https://github.com/eddyndumia/lipa-mdogo-mdogo)
