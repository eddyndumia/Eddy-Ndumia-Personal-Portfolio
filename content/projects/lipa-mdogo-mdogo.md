A Node and Express backend for "lipa mdogo mdogo" financing, where someone pays for a phone or other big item in small pieces over time. It tracks each plan and the repayments made against it.

**Stack:** Node.js, Express

**Repo:** [lipa-mdogo-mdogo](https://github.com/eddyndumia/lipa-mdogo-mdogo)
