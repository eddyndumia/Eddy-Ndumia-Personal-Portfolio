A trading timer and journal built for forex traders who need precision timing around high-impact economic news releases. PropFire gives real-time EST-based countdowns for selected trading days, a full trade journal with P&L tracking, and equity-curve visualization — removing the guesswork from systematic news trading.

**Tech stack:** Python

**Repo:** [prop-fire](https://github.com/eddyndumia/prop-fire)
