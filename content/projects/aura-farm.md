Slang falls from the top of the screen. You slide under the Ws, RIZZ and HIM and dodge the Ls, MID, CRINGE and NPC. A RATIO costs two hearts. Chain catches and a combo multiplier kicks in, and the more aura you farm in one run the more your character evolves, through the Italian brainrot cast from TikTok, Brr Brr Patapim, Tralalero Tralala, Ballerina Cappuccina, Cappuccino Assassino, up to Tung Tung Tung Sahur.

Around the run there's the stuff that brings people back the next day: 12 characters to unlock, upgrades for the shield, magnet and hearts, three daily missions, a 7-day streak, and a toggle that swaps the words for Sheng.

It's TypeScript on a plain canvas, no game engine, so the same code runs in the browser and as an Android app through Capacitor. Ads are AdMob, rewarded first (revive, double aura, free chest) with interstitials capped hard so they don't kill retention. Because kids play games like this, the first screen asks for a birth year and under-13s only get child-directed, non-personalised ads, per Google Play's Families policy. The character art is public domain from Wikimedia Commons, cut out of its backgrounds with a segmentation model. Drops are pre-rendered sprites and the game lowers its own resolution if a phone can't keep up.

**Status:** live, v1.1.1, web and Android APK. Google Play after the 14-day closed test, mid October.
